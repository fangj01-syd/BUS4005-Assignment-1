## Summary

**4 of 5 checks pass.** Finding: “jobs in progress” is ambiguous. The model counted only jobs whose status is literally “in progress”, so the table hides that Sam has 6 open jobs — exactly what the partner needs to see. **Next version:** “Table of open jobs (any status except lodged) per staff member.”
