# P02 · Missing-document reminder

**Current version:** v1.2 · **Status:** Tested · **Workflow stage:** Onboarding 2 · **Feeds into:** —

> Sample portfolio for BUS4005 Assessment 1. Harbourside Accounting and all figures are invented. Use the structure, not the content.

## Prompt text

```text
You are a client services officer at Harbourside Accounting. Write a short
reminder to [CLIENT_FIRST_NAME] about documents still missing for their
[FINANCIAL_YEAR] return. Received: [RECEIVED_LIST]. Still missing:
[MISSING_LIST]. This is reminder [REMINDER_NUMBER] of 3. Appointment:
[APPOINTMENT_DATE]. Upload link: [PORTAL_LINK].
Under 120 words. Thank them for what has arrived, then list ONLY the items in
[MISSING_LIST]. Reminder 1: friendly. Reminder 2: friendly and mentions the
appointment date. Reminder 3: politely says the appointment may need to move.
Never mention penalties or the ATO.
```

## Workflow / task

Onboarding, step 2. Triggered 7, 14 and 21 days after P01 if the document tracker still shows missing items.

## Problem solved

Chasing documents takes several staff hours a week in peak season and is often forgotten. Each rescheduled appointment wastes about 45 minutes of an accountant’s time.

## Automation potential

Very high. Reminders 1–2 can send automatically with an audit log; reminder 3 is checked by a person because it affects the client relationship.

## Risks & limitations

Listing a document already received → “ONLY the items in [MISSING_LIST]” and the tracker as the single source of truth. Pushy tone → tone set per reminder, human check on reminder 3.

## Version history

> Earlier versions are illustrative: they show how a version log should read, but only the current version was run in testing.

| Version | Change made | What happened | Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|
| v1.0 | Reminder listing [MISSING_LIST] | Listed an item already received in 2 of 9 cases | The AI merged both lists |
| v1.1 | Added “list ONLY the items in [MISSING_LIST]” | 0 of 9 errors | Name the single source of truth |
| v1.2 | Set tone per [REMINDER_NUMBER]; no penalties or ATO | Real test: 6 of 6 pass | Tone can be a rule, not a hope |

## Test evidence

**Real test** — Claude Opus 5.5, 22 November 2026, current version only. 6 of 6 pass (2 clients × reminders 1–3, 71–111 words). Received items were never listed as missing; no ATO or penalty wording. Full inputs and outputs: [tests/P02.md](../tests/P02.md).
