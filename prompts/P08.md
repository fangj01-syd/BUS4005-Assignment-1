# P08 · Overdue fee reminder

**Current version:** v1.3 · **Status:** Tested · **Workflow stage:** Accounts receivable · **Feeds into:** —

> Sample portfolio for BUS4005 Assessment 1. Harbourside Accounting and all figures are invented. Use the structure, not the content.

## Prompt text

```text
You are the accounts officer at Harbourside Accounting. Write a polite but
firm payment reminder to [CLIENT_NAME] for invoice [INVOICE_NO] of [AMOUNT],
now [DAYS_OVERDUE] days overdue. Original due date: [DUE_DATE]. Payment
options: [PAYMENT_OPTIONS].
Under 150 words, Australian English. Include the amount, due date, how to pay,
and who to contact if they think the invoice is wrong. Do not threaten legal
action or debt collection. If more than 60 days overdue, add one sentence
offering a payment plan.
```

## Workflow / task

Accounts receivable. Triggered at 14, 30 and 60 days overdue; the accounts officer checks and sends, the partner approves anything over 60 days.

## Problem solved

About 1 in 8 invoices is paid late, and reminders are inconsistent or not sent in peak season, which hurts a small practice’s cash flow.

## Automation potential

High. All inputs are in the accounting software and the message is routine.

## Risks & limitations

Reminder for an invoice already paid or disputed → runs only after daily bank reconciliation; disputed invoices excluded. Damaging a long relationship → no legal threats, payment-plan offer, partner approval at 60+ days.

## Test evidence

6 cases (14, 30, 60 and 90 days overdue): the payment-plan sentence appeared only above 60 days, 6 of 6.
