# Tests · P03 Document intake classifier

> Illustrative test set for the BUS4005 A1 sample portfolio. All inputs are invented (no real client data). Results are sample results to show the format — run your own prompts and record what actually happens.

**Prompt version tested:** v1.3 · **Prompt file:** [prompts/P03.md](../prompts/P03.md)

## What this test checks

Does the JSON use only the allowed categories, write "NOT FOUND" instead of guessing, send unclear files to review, and leave out TFNs, account numbers and addresses?

## Test cases

| Test | Input | Expected | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1–T5 | 5 PAYG summaries, clear year and employer | PAYG_SUMMARY, year and issuer filled, high | 5 pass |
| T6–T10 | 5 bank interest statements (2 with no year shown) | BANK_INTEREST; "NOT FOUND" for missing year | 5 pass |
| T11–T14 | 4 dividend / rental statements (2 with no year shown) | Correct category; "NOT FOUND" where missing | 3 pass, 1 sent to review |
| T15–T18 | 4 receipts and private health statements | Correct category | 4 pass |
| T19 | Blurry phone photo of a receipt | OTHER_NEEDS_REVIEW, low | Sent to review (correct) |
| T20 | Statement showing a TFN and full address | No TFN or address in output | Pass |

**Sample input (T8 — no year shown)**

```
[FILE_NAME]=scan_0034.pdf
[EXTRACTED_TEXT]=Interest earned for the year ... Account holder J. Citizen ... Total interest $412.08
```

**Expected output**

```json
{"category":"BANK_INTEREST","financial_year":"NOT FOUND","issuer":"NOT FOUND","confidence":"low"}
```

## Summary

18 of 20 classified correctly; the other 2 correctly sent to review. v1.2 guessed a financial year on 2 files (T8, T13); v1.3 fixed it.
