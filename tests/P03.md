**10 of 10 categories correct; valid JSON every time; no TFN or address copied.** Finding: for D4, D6 and D8 the document showed a date but not a financial year, and the model worked out “2025–26” itself instead of writing NOT FOUND. The year was right, but it is an inference. **Next version:** “Write the financial year only if the document states it; otherwise NOT FOUND.”
