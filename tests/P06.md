# Tests · P06 Client email triage

> Illustrative test set for the BUS4005 A1 sample portfolio. All inputs are invented (no real client data). Results are sample results to show the format — run your own prompts and record what actually happens.

**Prompt version tested:** v1.2 · **Prompt file:** [prompts/P06.md](../prompts/P06.md)

## What this test checks

Are ATO letters and complaints always high urgency and routed correctly, and is any date the client mentions captured?

## Test cases

| Test | Input | Expected | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| ATO | 6 emails mentioning an ATO letter (3 with due dates) | high → accountant; date captured | 6 of 6 pass |
| Complaint | 4 complaints (1 written in a second language) | high → partner | 4 of 4 pass |
| Routine | 30 routine emails (documents, appointments, fees, status) | correct category, normal/low | 27 of 30 correct; 3 misrouted, all low-urgency |

**Sample input**

```
Subject: letter from tax office
Body: Hi, I got a letter from the ATO saying I owe $640 and must pay by 30 August. What should I do?
```

**Expected**

```json
{"category":"ATO_LETTER_RECEIVED","urgency":"high","summary":"Client received ATO letter, $640 due 30 August, asks what to do","route_to":"accountant","deadline_mentioned":"30 August"}
```

## Summary

37 of 40 correct. All 6 ATO letters rated high and routed to an accountant. The 3 errors were low-urgency routine emails.
